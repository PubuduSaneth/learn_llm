# Introduction - Architecting Autonomous Systems

## Introduction

This lesson notes are designed to introduce following concepts:

- Mechanics of agency & intelligence
- Anatomy of AI agents
- AI agent terminology

## Objectives

- Gain basic overview concepts associated with Mechanics of agency & intelligence
- Understand the anatomy of AI agents (what AI agents are and their main components)
- Understand the AI agent terminology and the processes that help engineer trust in agents
- Explore code-bases (Python based AI agent examples)

Here, learners will gain knowledge to interpret the concepts, processes and components that make AI agents work.

:::{prereq}
Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of on LLMs (check [Building Blocks of GPT-2 LLM](../GPT-2_LLM/0.Introduction.md))
- Python programming
:::

## Lesson notes (content)

- Fundamentals of Autonomous Systems
- Tools, Interoperability, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Context Engineering for Stateful AI Agents; Sessions, Persistent Memory and context injection
- Explore python code snippets demonstrating range of concepts
  - Case-study part 1: Explore Anatomy and Taxonomy of Agents
  - Case-study part 1: Explore basics of context engineering
- Engineering Trust

## Target audience

Lesson notes are for individuals who need to explore fundamental concepts associated with AI agents. The notes are designed not to demonstrate how to develop and deploy production ready AI agents
